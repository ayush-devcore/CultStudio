Privacy notice

CultStudio uses Formspree to receive and process project enquiries submitted through this website.

Information collected

The form sends your name, email address, optional business name, selected service, optional budget, project description and consent response to Formspree. Please do not include passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

How the information is used

CultStudio uses the enquiry to respond about your project. Formspree processes the submission and stores it in the connected form account. Email notifications depend on that form’s settings. Submission retention depends on the Formspree plan and account settings.

Third-party processing

Formspree is an external service provider. Its own privacy policy explains how it handles personal information, including international processing and retention: Formspree Privacy Policy.

Questions or deletion requests

Use the project enquiry form to contact CultStudio about information submitted through this website. Requests relating to data held by Formspree may also need to be made under its privacy policy.
